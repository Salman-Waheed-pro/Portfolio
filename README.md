# Portfolio

Personal site of Salman Waheed, software engineering student at TAMK (Tampere, Finland).

**Live:** https://salman-waheed-pro.github.io/Portfolio/

![Screenshot of the portfolio home page](assets/screenshot.jpg)

## What's here

- `index.html`: the current site, one file, no framework or build step.
- `assets/work/`: screenshots of the projects, shown inside each project's screen.
- `assets/vendor/`: d3-geo, topojson-client and world map data for the small globe (licences in `NOTICE.md`).
- `work/asten-coach-finder.html`: the interactive solution page from a workshop.
- `v1/`, `v2/`, `original/`: earlier versions, kept as they were and hidden from search engines.

The top of the page is a field of lines. Leave it alone and it calms into a desert rose; move fast or scroll and it storms again. A sentence then lights up word by word as you scroll. How I work opens on a line from Pascal beside Thomas Cole's The Course of Empire (public domain). The quote fades when the first habit scrolls into view, and each column rises with its habit, standing fully once that habit reaches the middle of the screen. The fourth puts the roof on. The globe in Background flies to Tampere and raises a Finnish flag. In the work list, opening a project raises a browser screen: public projects click through from the picture, private ones scroll for the details. The footer line sends a small hug to whoever hovers it.
